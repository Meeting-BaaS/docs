    - `certificate_not_expired`: the certificate is within its validity period (`warn` when it expires within 30 days).
    - `workspace_active`: the workspace `state` is `active`.
    - `has_active_login`: at least one login on the workspace has `state` `active`.
    - `logins_match_domain`: every login email is on the workspace `domain` or a subdomain of it (`warn` otherwise).

    Each check has a `status` of `pass`, `fail` or `warn` and a `message`. `ok` is `true` when no check has status `fail`; warnings do not affect it.
